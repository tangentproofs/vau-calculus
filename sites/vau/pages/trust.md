## The explanations

- **Drafted by Claude (AI).** No person has reviewed them. The authors of the cited works have not seen
  them. Every node is `review: draft`.
- **Sources.** Definitions follow the extracted text of Shutt's thesis PDF (chapters 4, 8, 9, 13-15) and
  his 2007 NEPLS talk script. Schumacher's posts were read for the environment and actor views. Wand's
  1998 paper is summarized from its well-known result and from Shutt's discussion of it in thesis
  chapter 15; this site does not quote it.
- **Quotes.** Only short phrases are quoted, each with its source. The thesis is not copied into this
  repository.
- **Worked examples** (for example the reduction trace on the
  [ReductionSchemata](#/node/ReductionSchemata) node) are this site's. They illustrate the rules; they are
  not evidence of any property.
- **Comments** on the nodes use GitHub Discussions (giscus, category Announcements) on this repository.
